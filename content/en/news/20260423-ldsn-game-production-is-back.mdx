## Latest DyingStar News (LDSN :D)

Here's an update on the project's progress over the past few days.

## Introduction

**Game production is finally back on track!**

After several months of debating and writing the [GVD](https://developer.dyingstar-game.com/docs/project/GVD/) and the [GCD](https://developer.dyingstar-game.com/docs/project/GCD/introduction/), we're now starting to write the [GDD](https://developer.dyingstar-game.com/docs/project/GDD/introduction/), which contains / will contain the details of the game.

The GDD will take some time as it will be packed with information (game details); we've already started writing it with the first playable version in mind ([MVP](https://developer.dyingstar-game.com/docs/project/GCD/scope/)).

As a result, we're bringing back weekly news to keep you up to date on what's happening, because things are happening!

## 📖 GDD

On the GDD side, several pages have been written over the past few days:

- descriptions of the planets and moons of the first planetary system, [Tarsis](https://developer.dyingstar-game.com/docs/project/GDD/worldbuilding/5_01_geographie/system_tarsis/system_tarsis/)
- description and illustration of the [first biomes](https://developer.dyingstar-game.com/docs/project/GDD/worldbuilding/5_01_geographie/biome/active_volcano/)
- description of how [QGIS is used to create planets](https://developer.dyingstar-game.com/docs/planetTech/qgis/) (elevation, biomes, roads, rivers...)
- [game directory structure](https://developer.dyingstar-game.com/docs/creativeConcept/files_structure/) for 3D models, textures and Godot scenes. Not yet applied to the repository, but it's coming in a few days.
- [Creating models in Blender](https://developer.dyingstar-game.com/docs/creativeConcept/3d_models/), specifics, exporting to Godot

## 🧍 Character creation

We're currently reviewing the character specifications (GDD), and they'll be published in a few days.

We want to kick off the creation of a character, which includes 3D modeling, animation, sounds...

To that end, I'm proposing a meeting on **Wednesday, April 29 at 9:00 PM**.
The goal is to work out with those in the know how to break down character creation and create the tasks, or even assign them.

The meeting has been created, you can sign up here: [Discord event](https://discord.gg/Sp7W9eDz?event=1496628659019059272)
