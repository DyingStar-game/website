## Results of the First NPC Vote

To start with, we'll have 5 NPCs. They'll work in shifts so that there are 2 people at the warehouse during the day and 1 person at night.

As for the shifts, Sandbox (Tarsis IV) completes a full rotation in 25 hours, so night time will gradually shift from one day to the next. The narrative team has put together a table of the NPC shifts (image attached to this news). It's not final yet, but it's close.
