## 🚀 What's new

- **Social** (1st iteration): profile, friends, missions, corps, groups, economy/wallet
- **Universe simulation**: goods (ore, food…) simulated per village and warehouse, **recalculated every 5 min**. In case of a shortage, an **automatic mission** is generated (can be accepted, not yet completed)
- **Launcher v1.0** (thanks Anthony): social features working (friends, corp)
- 🧊 **Feature freeze**: until Monday, **bugs and optimization only**

## 🚚 Vehicles and batteries

- **Engine** and **Battery** wiki pages added (sub-pages of Components)
- Bug fixed: **Godot's default engine braking**, which made 2 engines faster than 1
- Now **103 km/h max** (empty, on flat ground) with 1 to 3 engines, only **acceleration** changes
- ⚠️ Road roughness is at **0.5** versus **0.9** for the ground, so some slopes are harder on the road than next to it. Proposal: switch to 1 (ddurieux is looking into it)
- 🔋 Recharging on the ground markings at **garages**: about **8 min from 0 to 100%**
- A garage in every town/village, **factories every ~47 km** (versus ~250 before)
- New game design channel: **battery charger** (in-game item for the MVP)

## 🗺️ Map and level design

- Main plateau updated with the real colors
- Materials spreadsheet opened (sandbox)
- Village: buildings and street furniture defined, 3D handoff coming soon
- 📌 **List of 3D assets** pinned in the creative forum. Additions go through **WarpZone**
- New 3D modelers have joined 🎉 Street lamps validated (designer look for big cities, industrial look for mining villages)
- ~60-70 villages, ~20,000 spawn points

## 🐛 Bug reporting process

- **One post per bug** in the **QA-Report** forum, see [the news](https://discord.com/channels/1399325839665004554/1428454919861702726/1559664832431530115)
- **Screenshot including the right-hand panel** (lat/long) for holes, stuttering, etc.
- Emoji reactions (duplicate, fixed…) still to be defined and explained
- Known bug: roads over canyons add dirt instead of building a bridge (fix in progress)

## 🖥️ Monday the 19th test: Server Meshing Dynamic

- Goal: **75 players/server**, then maybe 100-130
- The point is to measure **server load and network**
- ⚠️ **Teleporters do not work between servers**
- **NPCs** will fill in if we're short on players. The more the better, so spread the word! (stream possible with Hugo)
