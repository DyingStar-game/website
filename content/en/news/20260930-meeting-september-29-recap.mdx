## 🚀 What's new

- Website: news published, Play button, big SEO boost
- FR/EN UI, screens fixed, footstep sounds
- **F2** starmap · **F7** screenshot without UI · **F8** screenshot with UI
- Graphics settings now visible in real time from the menu
- Volcanoes + lava flows, chunk loading almost instantaneous
- Orbital station with teleporter + EVA
- A 2,000 km highway (≈9,000 housing units to come) + a 42,000 km rail around the planet
- Truck: speed limiter, automatic wheel return

## 🔋 Battery

Consumption based on torque (0–600). Upcoming meeting with Bardok and Jarran to finalize the T1, then engine and slope tests.

## 🏗️ Lore

- Construction company = dedicated sub-branch (cities, infrastructure)
- Housing: containers at first → houses → residences, through reputation
- New corundum-based materials: armored glass, fibers for clothing/armor
- Inspiration images to be archived in the tech doc

## 🗺️ Terrain

Rough global elevation + plateaus/mountains/cliffs layered on top. Corundum colors + soil types per zone. Natural obstacles to go around (cliffs, volcano, radiation).

## 🧪 Test feedback

- Steering too twitchy: more range, progressive steering, reduced at high speed
- The starmap doesn't follow the planet's rotation → a feedback channel needs to be created
- Performance issues and crashes on AMD → debug via client.ini + logs

## 🏠 Buildings

Dynamic terrain flattening, adding villages without a reboot. Cases to test: canyon, underground, slopes. Physical elevator going into testing soon. Housing to be picked up again on the lore side (containers first).
