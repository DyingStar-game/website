## Launcher and game download 🚀

Hear ye, hear ye!

You can now download the launcher.

Once installed, you'll need to log in (via your Discord account) and download the game so you're ready for **tonight's test at 8:30 PM**.

The launcher is available for:

- **Windows**: [Dying_Star_Launcher.Setup.0.2.2.exe](https://github.com/DyingStar-game/launcher/releases/download/v0.2.2/Dying_Star_Launcher.Setup.0.2.2.exe)
- **Linux**:
  - rpm (Red Hat, Fedora...): [dying-star-launcher-0.2.2.x86_64.rpm](https://github.com/DyingStar-game/launcher/releases/download/v0.2.2/dying-star-launcher-0.2.2.x86_64.rpm)
  - deb (Debian, Ubuntu, Mint...): [dying-star-launcher_0.2.2_amd64.deb](https://github.com/DyingStar-game/launcher/releases/download/v0.2.2/dying-star-launcher_0.2.2_amd64.deb)
  - AppImage (universal format): [Dying_Star_Launcher-0.2.2.AppImage](https://github.com/DyingStar-game/launcher/releases/download/v0.2.2/Dying_Star_Launcher-0.2.2.AppImage)

Thanks to MaestriX, who worked tirelessly on the launcher (and put up with all my wild requests 🤣), as well as to everyone who was able to test it and report bugs (yes, we've already gone through several versions) 🔥
