## ⚙️ Engine & map

- **NPCs are ready**, but their release is **pushed back to Thursday evening**: performance diagnosis in progress (missing LODs on rocks, in particular).
- All **residential buildings are now placed directly on the planet** (no more base plate). Containers and crawlers not migrated yet.
- **Terrain resolution: 42 km → 2 km.** Much more relief, planet size 166 MB → 650 MB.
- **Roads drawn in QGIS → automatically exported into the engine.** Width is defined as a property (4 m for now, adjustable, it adapts on its own in-game).
- Automatic bridges over canyons: coded, **not sure they'll be ready for Thursday**.
- Rock crusher → machine → crate → warehouse chain: working.
- ⚠️ **Scale bug in QGIS**: the scalebar is wrong (3 km shown instead of 12-15). Lead: version difference (3.44 stable vs. a more recent one). To be confirmed.

## 🗺️ The demo map

- 4 villages/towns + one factory, one warehouse per village, 12-15 km roads, switchbacks on the mountain to test the relief.
- Two big cities sit in the basin, **beneath the dust** → access via tunnel or canyon only. The capital is on the same plateau but very far away.
- **Tunnels**: we'll need to add a "tunnel" road type, split into segments, with elevation data.
- Pure corundum ground = white, looks burnt out when the star is at its zenith. We'll make sure there's colour variety between regions (no vegetation to break it up).

## 🛣️ Guardrails & wind protection — open debate

- **bitogno**: crosswinds + dust require protection, especially at high speed. Walls don't necessarily have to hug the road or be permanent, only in windy areas.
- **Kainan**: a road closed on both sides = a permanent corridor, one player parks sideways and blocks everything. A game design problem to anticipate.
- **ddurieux**: the guardrails in the lore doc were mainly meant for the big highways. Possible test with a wall at ~200 m (but a 400 m footprint), or find an anti-wind mechanism that doesn't close off the road.
- Blocking will remain possible anyway in tunnels, on bridges, in canyons and at building entrances.

👉 **We'll continue on the forum, send in your proposals.**

## 🚧 Griefing & moderation (not settled)

Ideas raised: bans + a report system, patrolling NPCs and tow trucks, unblocking missions given to players, despawning a vehicle that has been stationary for ~3 min (but how do you tell a genuine breakdown apart?). Reservations raised: this assumes 24/7 moderation, and giving players authority comes with its own abuses.

## 🪧 Road signs

- 3-4 reusable 3D shapes (triangle, square, octagon), only the texture changes. The half-perforated signs won unanimous approval.
- Clarification: the "spinning" sign **does not rotate**; it tilts on a spring in the direction of the wind so it doesn't get eaten away by the corundum, then springs back into position.
- Size to be set according to the vehicles' max speed, then **test readability in-game** (real-world standards = a source of inspiration, not a rule).
- US style (yellow) vs. European: left to the art team. Reservation: yellow won't read well on light-coloured ground.

## 📚 Lore & documentation

- The "customs / behaviours" topic is open and being filled in; "data management on missions" will be closed for lack of feedback.
- Foods added, Kelvin values corrected, big cities added to the map, elevation work in progress.
- Inconsistency to fix: the doc explains why there are no robots… while there are some in-game.
- **Vehicle design**: no written style guide, the old docs seem to be lost. We'd go with **guiding keywords** (industrial, boxy shapes) rather than an imposed theme.
