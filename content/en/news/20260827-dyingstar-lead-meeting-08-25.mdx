## ⚙️ Infra & server

- New version deployed last Thursday: the major performance issue is **fixed**, the Godot server now runs at around **60 TPS**
- Infra migration completed Sunday/Monday → we are **100% on the new infra in production**
- Slimmed-down dev images: no more need to rebuild all of Horizon for every config change
- Rock rework in progress: spawn zones, consistent size/weight, breaking into small pieces (not just 2 or 4)
- Test with ~95 players: no more issues with other players' possession 🎉, but a few packets not getting through → Horizon optimizations in progress, new test within a few days
- Bridges to fix (step + sliding) before extending the road to the big city and making the switchback road testable → targeted for next week
- No admin mode yet, the topic is on the table

## 🔋 MVP components

- Priority confirmed: **battery, then engine**, with no sub-components for the MVP
- **4 slots** requested from the art team; batteries on the sides, engine in the component bay (under the dump bed on the current model) — no constraints imposed on placement
- The engine channel is open, the battery will come next (the engine needs to be defined first)

## 🛣️ Roads & speeds

- We're going with **3 road types → slow / medium / fast** rather than fixed numerical values; this will be adjusted in-game based on actual distances
- Open question: is it the vehicle that limits speed, or the road layout (turns, switchbacks)?
- A build with different road types and defined distances will be prepared for testing
- The debate continues in the dedicated channel, it's yours to take up 👀

## 🏗️ Vehicle elevator & canyons

- Kept as a **bonus** (outside the strict MVP): lets you bring back trucks that fell into the canyons and lays the groundwork for the elevator system on the code side
- Reminder: canyons weren't planned for the MVP but they're there, generated 300 m below ground level and connected to each other across the whole planet

## 🧩 Lore vs gameplay consistency

Big discussion about the level of realism for engines (tech, size, power). Consensus: we keep **universe consistency** — the foundation remains our world in the future — but it must stay **non-blocking** and not slow down development. We then simplify to keep it digestible.

## 🎨 Art & narrative

- Road signs added in Art, currently blocked by a lore question
- New narrative question opened: why don't we use AI on Ares?
- Ares design: the Figma information was lost, it needs to be recovered
- Holes in the ground on Tarsis in progress (very long); duplicate points double the map size → a cleanup script will be written

## 🗓️ Game design meetings

Agreement in principle for a recurring meeting (every 2 to 3 weeks), **with a topic defined in advance** to keep it productive. Time slots to be varied (weekday evenings, weekends) to reach more people. This doesn't replace the written channels.
