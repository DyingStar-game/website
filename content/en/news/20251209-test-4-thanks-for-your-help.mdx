I'd like to thank everyone who took part last night (and today too, since the server was still up and running).

It showed that many locks (thread / async deadlocks) have been eliminated, but some still remain. We managed a run of over 30 minutes without everyone getting stuck. There were some grey screen issues (investigation ongoing), and some players didn't have the planet's clouds...

The race to the orbital elevator was really cool.

I now have 1 GB of logs to go through (not counting the server log, which has been running non-stop since 11 pm yesterday and is currently at 35 GB).

The test was very instructive and the game is starting to become playable, which makes my heart go boom ❤️

Here are a few screenshots you took yesterday 🔥

I don't know yet when the next test will take place, stay tuned right here ^\_^
