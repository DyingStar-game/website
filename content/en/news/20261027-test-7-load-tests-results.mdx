## 📊 Test 7 load tests of October 26: the results

Thanks to everyone who showed up last night! Two tests were on the program:

- **Test 1**: ~35 players and up to 600 NPCs, villages of 50
- **Test 2**: ~30 players and 200 NPCs, villages of 25

**✅ What held up**

- The game servers: ~50 players per server at 60 tps, without flinching
- The central server (Horizon): 640 connections, CPU headroom to spare, no memory leak
- Dynamic server meshing works pretty well

**❌ What broke**

- 🔒 **Nobody could join anymore** (test 1, around 8:49 PM): a player whose connection was stuck reconnected, and that locked up Horizon. Only a restart could unblock it.
- 🧊 **Frozen players**, especially on voluptas: when a connection drops, Horizon holds on to everything it needs to send for 60 s before cutting it off. During that time, nothing moves on your end.
- 🐌 **Heavy lag as soon as the NPCs arrived**, but only for remote players: 20 ms before the NPCs, up to 1.7 s afterwards. Our main lead: NPC traffic (up to 224 Mbit/s) goes through the same internet connection.
- 💾 **The memory of the game servers (godot)** is never released after players leave.

**🔧 What's next**

- Fix the connection lockup
- Stop stacking up late positions: a player who drops will catch up all at once instead of staying frozen for a minute
- Move Horizon and the godot servers to a datacenter to get a real connection
- I'm preparing specifications for (to be published in about a week):
  - changing the network protocol (moving part of it to UDP to avoid lockups)
  - handling latency as best as possible for those on other continents (while we're at it)
