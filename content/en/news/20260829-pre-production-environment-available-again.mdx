The servers are available again so you can have some fun in the game following the shutdown ([see the announcement](https://discord.com/channels/1399325839665004554/1399737844830044241/1542251603334266951)).

Several fixes have been applied for the connection issues.

More fixes are still on the way in this area, but in the meantime you can play: the NPCs are there to store your crates in the warehouse, and Franck is still wandering around the planet. 🔥

**Quick tip:** to get across the first bridge with a truck, drive to the end and, once you're up against the step, get out and push the truck.

The second bridge is impossible to cross. Bridge fixes will be coming in a few days ^\_^
