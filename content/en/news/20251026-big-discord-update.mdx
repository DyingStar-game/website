## 🛠️ Big Discord Update!

Hello everyone 👋

The server has undergone a major overhaul!
You can find all the details in 👉 [the dedicated channel](https://discord.com/channels/1399325839665004554/1428449953482670120)

## 🔧 Main changes

- Some roles have been archived (and will soon be deleted, _in 1 week_).
- Many roles have been reviewed and adjusted → remember to check your roles from the server's "Channels & Roles" tab.
- All voice channels are now created via the voice channel creation channel.
- Teams are now visible from the general channels.
- To see the detailed channels, simply pick up the corresponding team role.
- If you have any issues or questions, contact your lead or drop by the help channel.

More information here: [https://discord.com/channels/1399325839665004554/1428449953482670120](https://discord.com/channels/1399325839665004554/1428449953482670120)
