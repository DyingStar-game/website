_Not a happy message, but a necessary one_

This week, our Audio lead left the project.

It could have ended there, but no 🙁

He deleted a good number of his messages and several forums. For the forums, it's quite a problem, because he deleted a lot of messages, sounds, music, videos... in short, contributions that some of you had provided 😭

It's truly disheartening to do that on an **open source, community-driven project**.

**I ask you above all not to take justice, revenge or whatever you might call it into your own hands; let's leave human foolishness in its own bubble.**

We keep moving forward, and we're trying to recover the audio parts here and there (we were already able to recover a small part of it yesterday).

For the messages from the gameplay, game design and lore sections, it's going to be more complex, but we'll do without or we'll redo them even better, **I have great faith in you** 🔥
