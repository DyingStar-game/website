## 🛠️ Tech progress

- **Truck**: new in-game model with **4 component slots**. Next steps: integrate the elevator and transcribe the engine formulas.
- **Roads**: highways with rock fusion and an Ares Transport-style waffle pattern, taking on the biome's color. A road can follow the terrain or, beyond a maximum slope, **dig a tunnel** 🕳️ (turns and junctions not handled yet).
- **Rail**: Creative Commons model simplified from ~35,000 down to **~3,600 triangles** (thanks The_Moye for the optimization).
- **Chunks**: purple "skirts" removed, better stitching between LOD 0→4. Still a few performance issues (and part of the atmosphere shader will be adjustable in the settings).
- **Entity streaming**: works well, much faster builds (no more gigabytes of elevation data bundled in).
- **Rocks**: 10 to 12,000 rocks on the server side tested without a hitch 💪
- **FPS bug fixed** 🎤: a LiveKit constant in seconds instead of milliseconds was making the game lag with the mic enabled.
- **Ground**: **hex tiling** shader (random rotation per tile), avoids repetitive patterns, with no performance cost.
- **Editor**: chunk loading just like in-game + a free-roam camera (thanks warpzone), ideal for level design 🗺️
- **Godot 4.8**: texture streaming is coming, a big FPS gain is expected even on low-end machines. Godot 4.x version upgrades remain painless.

## 🌍 PlanetTech

- bitogno fixed the Sandbox density values and added colored biome generation (areas more or less blue/red/green depending on the ore, with noise).
- Reference point added to the wiki for roads.

## ⚙️ Game design

- **T1 engine validated** (BARDOK's values): 100 electric / 600 torque → ~**103 km/h** top speed, 0-100 in **22-23 s**. A starting point, adjustable.
- **Battery**: dedicated channel opened. Leads: ~100 km per engine, swap frequency, avoiding "full batteries" making everything too easy.
- **Roads & driving**: the idea that the road type affects acceleration and sliding. The starter truck would be average everywhere.
- **Speed limiter**: debate in progress (toggleable or not, scroll wheel, memorizing the current speed…).
- **Vehicles in cities**: not decided yet. Leads: pedestrian cities, automatic restriction via zone markers, warehouses on the outskirts / right next to the elevator and the spaceport.

## ☢️ Atmosphere & danger

- 2% CO2 below the cloud + uranium/thorium in the ground: we want to avoid a starting planet that is deadly everywhere.
- Direction: **localized** dangers (high-concentration areas) that block the player without the proper equipment.
- **Datapad**: default readings (gravity, breathable air, radiation…).

## ⛏️ Resources

- We're keeping the current corundum types to stay simple (brown corundum for ferrous areas).
- Rocks will take on the color of their ore. Rock weight ratio to be applied this week.

## 🎯 MVP priorities

1. Houses & prefabs
2. Warehouse improvements (docks, shelves…)
3. Mining and transport missions (full loop or separate missions)
4. **A single vehicle** for now; we flesh out the gameplay before multiplying vehicles

Inventory and wallet: not a priority for the MVP (batteries and rocks are carried by hand).
