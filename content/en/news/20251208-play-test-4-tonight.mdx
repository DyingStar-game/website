## Play test #4 tonight!

No postponement this time (I don't fancy getting lynched in the public square :D).

**The play test is definitely happening tonight!**

Here's how it will go:

1. Meet at 9 PM in the voice channel (see [the Discord event](https://discord.com/events/1399325839665004554/1440457065926492324)).
2. I'll post a news item right here with the game download links (about 300 MB to download) around 9:00 - 9:15 PM, **Linux and Windows archives only**; you'll need to extract and run the program (shouldn't be too complicated 👍).
3. I'll share the scenarios each time; we have 4 spawn points with different things at each one, and we'll run various tests to evaluate the server load.

Some of you asked whether you could stream the test: it's possible if you have fewer than 50 viewers on your stream 😅, so not Hugo, eh!

**If the test works**, we may leave the servers up for several hours so that those who aren't available at 9 PM can try it out a bit.

Just for the record, this morning I ran a test on my machine while it was somewhat loaded, so not representative, but I got up to 454 players 🔥 🚀
