## Contributors

Someone pointed out to me this morning that it feels like I'm the only one working on the project. It can give that impression because I'm the one writing the news. **That is clearly not the case!**

As a reminder, we hold lead meetings every Tuesday evening, which lead to the news being published a few hours later with the project's progress. These meetings are **_public_** and you can also speak up if needed.

The project is about fifteen active people (see the infographic) over the last 6 months (that is, since game production resumed). Thanks to them for their time.

There are also **many one-off contributions** in all sorts of areas and I'd like to thank those people as well (hard to name them all because I'd forget some, but I'm thinking of every one of you)

While I'm at it, we could use:

- **1 animator** for the character
- **3D artists** to create the 3D assets; we have 2 people but that's not enough 😅
