## 📢 Lead meetings go public

You may already know this... or actually maybe not: every Tuesday at 9 PM there is a lead meeting to discuss organisation, progress in each department, issues encountered and various important topics.

Until now, these meetings were private while we ironed out how they work (which was far from simple).

From now on, these meetings will be public: you can attend on voice, but you won't be able to speak (except in the chat if you have questions and there's time left at the end to answer them).

So the next meeting is this Tuesday at 9 PM: [Discord event](https://discord.com/events/1399325839665004554/1431641313148801024/1437894652723200000)

See you Tuesday 🥳
