## 🛠️ Technical progress

- **Furnished containers**: beds, nightstands and a wardrobe. The shower/toilet cubicles were removed because they took up too much space and prevented NPCs from leaving the housing units.
- **Exploration missions working**: you place a point on the map with a reward debited from the creator's account. The mission validates automatically upon arrival in the area.
- **Admin panel**: routines and activities with calendars, a default schedule, rotation across 5 NPCs per position (25-hour day).
- **Rocks**: density reduced through a global factor (×30), which gives bulky blocks weighing less than 50 kg. This is easy to adjust.
- **Cliffs**: clearly there (around 800 m high, at the edge of the mountain area towards M0059).
- Several fixes made and performance improved (mirrors inside the containers).

## 🌐 Network test results

- ✅ **Server meshing (Horizon + Godot) is holding up well**.
- 🐛 Some players could no longer connect (an issue on the Horizon side); a fix is planned.
- ⚠️ **The lag comes from the client ↔ Horizon network**: some players had **more than 6 MB queued** for sending.
  - Main lead: switch **movement to UDP** so that only the latest values are sent. Two solutions are being studied.
  - Latency for players on other continents will be addressed at the same time.
  - There may be an anti-DDoS system on David's connection, hence a **datacenter test** to plan (Horizon + Godot servers). Cost: a few hundred €/month at most, around €20–30 for occasional use.
- 📊 With 25 players per village instead of 50, outgoing traffic drops significantly.
- 🔁 The network issue should be **reproducible with about 5 players and lots of NPCs**. A small test will be organised one evening once the fixes are in.
- 🛗 **Server switch inside the elevator**: there is a visible hiccup, but nothing broke.
- 📈 Reminder: the 4-day long-run test (ramping up to 30, with 1,200 NPCs) was stable.
- 🧠 To investigate: **Godot memory does not go back down** when the player count drops.
- 📰 A detailed report is available [here](https://discord.com/channels/1399325839665004554/1399737844830044241/1564711466312011887).

  - New naming convention.

  - **Farming villages confirmed** (biomass recycling).
- Work logbook and status promotions through missions, to be worked on with game design (Kainan, BARDOK).

  - Highlands: regolith dust or pebbles.

- **Minerals**: dedurium added and vanadium increased. David must provide the **list of components, minerals and foods** outside the code for validation. Minerals missing from the planet (such as water) are imported or come from the moons.

## ☀️ Ground too bright

- Kainan finds the white ground "blown out" and dazzling (around M001), which creates a comfort issue over a 25-hour cycle.
- David explains that this is normal for a very white, very smooth ground. He is going to **rework the ground dressing** (gravel, a less smooth surface) and the **material** (light reflection).
- It is not an MVP priority, but the point has been noted.
- We can still try to make it less bright.

## 🗑️ Returning and destroying items

- There is still no way to get rid of an item.
- Two options: a **bin or an "item deletion" grinder**, or a **vending machine that takes the equipment back** at the end of a mission, with a whitelist of accepted items.
- No automatic despawn is planned.

## 🖱️ Controls
