Hear ye, hear ye!!!!

This Sunday at 4 PM, 5 people will be joining Sandbox.

**Ygind Tars**, **Astréos**, **Alain Deloin**, **Crate Roberts** and **Jean Caisse** will welcome you at the warehouse this Sunday at 4 PM. They won't all be there at the same time: they work in shifts according to their work schedule.

The rest of the time, they'll be busy eating, doing cleaning tasks (not implemented yet, but simulated) and sleeping (yes, you sleep a little sometimes too, don't you?).

## What will happen in the warehouse?

The crates produced by the conveyor belt from the rocks you, fellow players, break and drop off can be brought to the warehouse and handed over to our friends so they can take care of storing them.

The warehouse can hold **4,608 crates**. I'm sure you'll fill it up for me in no time, and I'm quite curious to see how long it takes (oh! a challenge).

Of course, rock and crate spawning will be disabled, so you'll have to:

- find the rocks,
- break them,
- carry them (on foot or in the truck) to the crushing machine that creates the crates,
- then carry those to the warehouse.

You can sign up, which lets you join voice chat and also get a reminder for the event: [sign up for the event](https://discord.gg/yQBj8yUd?event=1537571983284240585)
